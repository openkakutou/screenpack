# Default screenpack assets

Every asset of the default screenpack, its author and its license. All of them can be redistributed with `game`.

Images and sounds are original, made by `tools/default-assets/generate.py` (sounds then encoded to OGG Vorbis with ffmpeg, see `tools/default-assets/README.md`); they are released, like the rest of this repository, under the MIT license (`LICENSE` at the repository root). The fonts are Noto Sans, unmodified, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`).
